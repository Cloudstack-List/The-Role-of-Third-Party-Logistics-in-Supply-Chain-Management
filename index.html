Understanding Third Party Logistics in Supply Chain Management

Third party logistics companies provide outsourced logistics services, including warehousing, transportation, inventory management, and order fulfillment. They act as an extension of your business, managing the logistics process so you can focus on growth and customer engagement.

However, many businesses struggle with common supply chain challenges such as:

  • Inventory inaccuracies are causing stockouts or overstock

  • Lengthy order processing times are delaying delivery

  • Lack of visibility across multiple sales channels

  • Rising fulfillment costs and inefficient warehouse operations

  • Complexity managing returns and shopper experiences


These issues can harm your brand reputation and customer satisfaction.
